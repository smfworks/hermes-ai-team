# Group-Chat Pod Manifest — Research Pod

A pod is a group chat in Hermes Desktop Bot Mode that organizes a sub-division of
the team around an area of focus. Rooms deliberate; the board records commitments.

## Purpose
Decide which papers, launches, and competitor moves deserve a vault note — and
who writes it.

## Area of focus
- Research scans, paper deep-dives, market moves, source quality

## Members (2–6)
| Bot | Role | Why in this pod |
|-----|------|-----------------|
| Atlas | Research analyst | Owns scans and vault notes |
| Quill | Content strategist | Flags what is publishable later |
| Chief of Staff | Coordinator | Keeps decisions on the board |

If more than 6 Bots want in, split the pod. A room >6 gets noisy; the hard cap
protects it.

## Standing agenda
- What landed this week that is worth a note
- Which notes are thin or unverified
- What to escalate to the operator (`@user`) — usually a publish or spend decision

## Coordination rules
- Use the board for commitments; use the room for deliberation.
- @-mention to target one member; mention nobody to get the round.
- A pod decision that needs to persist becomes a kanban card. Reference the
  card ID in the room.

## Creation
1. Hermes Desktop → Bots tab → New Group Chat → name it `Research Pod`.
2. Add the members above (local Bots, or from registered Connections for
   cross-machine).
3. Pin a link to this manifest in a message so members can cite it.
