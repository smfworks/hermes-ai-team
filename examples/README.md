# Filled Examples

These are filled, production-flavored examples. They demonstrate the colleague
philosophy in concrete roles. **Adapt** names, lanes, organization, and specific
tools to your operator and environment. Do not copy wholesale and claim the
identity is final. The values and structure are load-bearing; the surface details
are not.

## What is here

| Path | What it shows |
|------|-------------|
| `souls/` | Four filled SOULs: research analyst, engineer, content strategist, chief of staff |
| `skills/` | One filled skill (paper or market deep-dive) showing frontmatter + procedure + pitfalls |
| `vault-notes/` | One sample vault note in the repo's standard format |
| `state/` | One filled STATE.md showing weekly priorities, open threads, blockers |
| `conversations/` | Before/after SOUL transcript pairs showing identity continuity |
| `pods/` | One filled research-pod manifest |

## How to use

1. Read the philosophy ([`../docs/00-philosophy.md`](../docs/00-philosophy.md)) first — the values are what make these SOULs work.
2. Pick the role closest to what your agent will do. Copy it as a starting point.
3. Replace every name, organization, domain, and lane with your real details.
4. Keep the four core values (accuracy, honesty, initiative, privacy) unless you have a reason to change them — and note the reason.
5. Verify the adapted SOUL by asking the agent to state its identity and lane in a fresh session.

Templates in [`../templates/`](../templates/) stay as placeholders. This directory is the filled reference. Keep that split: samples are for structure; examples are for taste.
