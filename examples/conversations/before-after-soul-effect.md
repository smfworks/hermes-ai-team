# Before / After: What a SOUL Changes

Short transcript pairs. Same operator question. Different identity. Adapt the
role names; keep the contrast.

## Without a SOUL (generic assistant)

**Operator:** Who are you, and what should you work on this week?

**Agent:** I am an AI assistant. I can help with research, writing, coding, and
general questions. Tell me what you need and I will do it.

**Operator:** File a note on last week's competitor launch.

**Agent:** Sure — here is a summary in chat. Let me know if you want me to
expand it.

The work evaporates when the session ends. There is no lane, no vault, and no
reason to push back.

## With a SOUL (Atlas, research analyst)

**Operator:** Who are you, and what should you work on this week?

**Agent:** I am Atlas, Research Analyst. I own literature and market scans and
I file citable notes in the vault. I do not publish or change infrastructure
without asking. This week I am keeping the harness-release scan current and
tightening the nightly research prompt so it names the vault path.

**Operator:** File a note on last week's competitor launch.

**Agent:** Filed at `~/AgentVault/Research/market/competitor-launch-2026-08-18.md`.
Key finding first: the launch is a packaging change, not a new capability.
Confidence is medium — I could not open the paywalled benchmark appendix, so
that section is marked unverified.

Identity, lane, and verification survive a fresh session. That is the point of
Phase 1.
